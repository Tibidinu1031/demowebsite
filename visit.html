Before you ask

The questions we actually get

If yours isn’t here, put it in the box above. We would rather answer it before you come than during.

What actually happens on a visit?

Marguerite meets you at the door at ten and walks you through both rooms and the garden while they are in use. You will be in the way, and that is fine. There is no presentation, no folder and no slideshow. At the end you sit in the kitchen with a coffee and ask everything you want to ask, for as long as it takes.

How does the waitlist work?

Strictly in date order, with one exception: siblings of current children go first. We do not charge to join it and we do not hold places back. If you are offered a place you have two weeks to decide, and saying no does not remove you from the list for later.

Do you provide food, and what about allergies?

Everything is cooked here by Ana: breakfast at 8:15, lunch at 12:15, and snacks at 2:30 and 3:45. It is all in the fee. We are a nut-free site and have run dairy-free, gluten-free and vegan alongside the main menu for years. Tell us on the form and we will talk it through properly before you start.

Which schools do you collect from?

Marlow Elementary, Beckett Road and St. Aldate’s, all on foot, leaving here at 2:55. If your school is not on the list, ask — if it is within about fifteen minutes’ walk we will look at adding it, and we have twice.

What happens when it rains?

We go out. We keep forty sets of waterproofs and about sixty boots in the hall in every size, so nobody misses the garden for want of a coat. Send a spare set of clothes and expect to be handed a bag on a Friday.

Does my child need to be out of diapers?

No. Roughly half the Nest is in diapers at any given time and we are entirely relaxed about it. We will follow whatever you are doing at home, and we will not start anything without asking you first.

What’s the sickness policy?

Forty-eight hours clear of vomiting or diarrhoea, twenty-four hours clear of a fever, and please keep them home for the first day of anything that looks contagious. We will call you if a child becomes unwell here, and we will not do it lightly — a runny nose is not a phone call.

When are you closed?

Forty-nine weeks a year. We close for three weeks in August, for the week between Christmas and New Year, and for five staff training days that we publish the previous June. Holiday Club runs through every other school holiday.

Can I see your policies?

They are on paper on the shelf by the door — safeguarding, behaviour, complaints, inclusion, medication, and the accident book. Ask and we will email you any of them, before or after a visit, without asking why.
